Assign by grid

For a few reviewers sharing all the papers, usually once submissions have closed. Tick a box to give that reviewer the paper, or untick it to take it back. A number is a score already given, which stays. "All" in a column ticks every paper for that reviewer except ones from their own school, which are flagged. A reviewer's own papers have no box. Nothing changes until you save.

Decide

Papers are listed by mean score, highest first. Authors don't see a decision until the decision emails go out (below). Once a paper's email has gone out, its decision can't be changed here.

Decision emails

The presenting author gets the email and coauthors are copied. In the wording, {name} is the presenting author, then {title}, {paper} (such as IT3), {program} (International Trade or Economic Theory), {meeting} (such as Spring 2027) and {where} (place and dates).

Send the decisions

Accept emails usually go first. Send the decline emails a few days later, once any accepted authors who can't come have withdrawn and their places are filled: until a paper's email goes out, its decision can still be changed. Authors without a decision see "Under review" after the deadline.
